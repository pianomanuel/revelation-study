Session 9: Revelation 21:1-22:5

For a participant: offer these when there is an opening, not as a lecture. Full session study

Parallel snippet, Rev 21:3

The Message 21:3
I heard a voice thunder from the Throne: “Look! Look! God has moved into ...
NIV 21:3
And I heard a loud voice from the throne saying, “Look! God’s dwelling place ...
NRSVue 21:3
And I heard a loud voice from the throne saying, “See, the home of ...

Evans: Evans quotes 21:4MSG | NIV | NRSVue as the believer's final home (Evans, 'Christ's Millennial Rule' (tonyevans.org blog)).

Agree or differ: Strong agreement.

Red marks the words of Jesus.

Insights to offer

1. Greek, 'new'. Kainos (21:5MSG | NIV | NRSVue) means new in quality: 'I am making all things new' suggests renewal of creation, not a replacement.

2. Greek, 'dwell'. Skēnōsei (21:3MSG | NIV | NRSVue), 'he will tabernacle', echoes John 1:14MSG | NIV | NRSVue and the covenant promise of Lev 26:11-12MSG | NIV | NRSVue.

3. Structure, the cube. The city is a cube (21:16MSG | NIV | NRSVue), the shape of the Holy of Holies (1 Kgs 6:20MSG | NIV | NRSVue): all God's people live in his presence, so there is no temple (21:22MSG | NIV | NRSVue).

4. OT echo, Eden. The river and tree of life (22:1-2MSG | NIV | NRSVue) come from Ezek 47MSG | NIV | NRSVue and Gen 2MSG | NIV | NRSVue: Eden restored and surpassed.

Steering questions

  • 21:4MSG | NIV | NRSVue promises no more tears. What loss are you carrying that this verse speaks to?
  • There is no temple because God and the Lamb are its temple (21:22MSG | NIV | NRSVue). How does God's presence change your view of ordinary work?
  • 'Nothing unclean will enter' (21:27MSG | NIV | NRSVue). How does that shape the way you fight temptation now?
  • What is restored here from Genesis 2-3? How does God dwelling with us change how we live now?

Bridging a tangent

  • On dates and headlines: 'Christians have read this differently for 2,000 years. What do all the views agree it asks of us?'
  • On a long debate: 'Whatever we decide about that, where does the text land for us this week?'
  • On a personal story: 'That connects to 21:3-4MSG | NIV | NRSVue. Can we look at it together?'
  • On fear or anxiety: 'Notice who is on the throne in this passage. It is written to steady us.'
  • Handing back: 'I'll stop there. [Leader], where did you want to go next?'

If someone asks

Is the new Jerusalem a literal city? Some futurists expect a literal cubic city about 1,400 miles on each side. Evans (inferred from his dispensational stance): a literal city, the eternal home of the redeemed.

Will this world be destroyed or renewed? 'I am making all things new' (21:5MSG | NIV | NRSVue) and kainos ('new in quality') lead many to favour renewal of the present creation (compare Rom 8:21MSG | NIV | NRSVue); others cite 2 Pet 3:10MSG | NIV | NRSVue and 21:1MSG | NIV | NRSVue ('passed away') for replacement.

Is there no sea in heaven? The sea in Revelation represents chaos, the source of the beast (13:1MSG | NIV | NRSVue), and Babylon's trade routes.
