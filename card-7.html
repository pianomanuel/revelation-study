Session 7: Revelation 15:1-19:5

For a participant: offer these when there is an opening, not as a lecture. Full session study

Parallel snippet, Rev 18:4

The Message 18:4
Doom to the City of DarknessFollowing this I saw another Angel descend from Heaven. ...
NIV 18:4
Warning to Escape Babylon’s JudgmentThen I heard another voice from heaven say: “‘Come out ...
NRSVue 18:4
Then I heard another voice from heaven saying, “Come out of her, my people, ...

Evans: Evans would apply 'come out' to separation from worldly systems (inferred from his dispensational, kingdom-agenda framework).

Agree or differ: Strong agreement on the ethical call; differ on Babylon's identity.

Red marks the words of Jesus.

Insights to offer

1. OT echo, 'come out'. 'Come out of her, my people' (18:4MSG | NIV | NRSVue) quotes Jer 51:45MSG | NIV | NRSVue, the call to exiles in Babylon. In Revelation it is an ethical and economic call.

2. Roman context, seven hills. The woman seated on seven hills (17:9MSG | NIV | NRSVue) points to Rome, the city of seven hills, shown on coins as the goddess Roma.

3. Structure, the cargo list. The merchants' cargo list (18:11-13MSG | NIV | NRSVue) moves from gold and silk to 'slaves, that is, human souls'. The critique is economic as well as religious.

4. Greek, 'Hallelujah'. 19:1-6MSG | NIV | NRSVue contains the only uses of 'Hallelujah' in the New Testament. Heaven celebrates justice for the victims, not destruction for its own sake.

Steering questions

  • Rome's cargo list ends with 'human souls' (18:13MSG | NIV | NRSVue). Where does our economy still trade in people?
  • 'Come out of her, my people' (18:4MSG | NIV | NRSVue). Name one practical way to 'come out' this month.
  • The merchants weep for lost profit, not for the people (18:11MSG | NIV | NRSVue). How do we keep money from shaping our grief and joy?
  • What makes Babylon attractive? What would 'come out of her' mean in our spending and time?

Bridging a tangent

  • On dates and headlines: 'Christians have read this differently for 2,000 years. What do all the views agree it asks of us?'
  • On a long debate: 'Whatever we decide about that, where does the text land for us this week?'
  • On a personal story: 'That connects to 18:4MSG | NIV | NRSVue. Can we look at it together?'
  • On fear or anxiety: 'Notice who is on the throne in this passage. It is written to steady us.'
  • Handing back: 'I'll stop there. [Leader], where did you want to go next?'

If someone asks

What and where is Armageddon? Har-Magedon is Hebrew for 'mountain of Megiddo', a site of famous battles (Judg 5:19MSG | NIV | NRSVue; 2 Kgs 23:29MSG | NIV | NRSVue), though Megiddo lies in a plain. Evans (inferred from his dispensational stance): a literal final battle in Israel.

Who is Babylon? Most scholars read Rome (seven hills, 17:9MSG | NIV | NRSVue; 'Babylon' as a code for Rome in 1 Pet 5:13MSG | NIV | NRSVue and Jewish texts), and by extension every idolatrous economic and political order. Evans (inferred from his dispensational stance): a future religious and economic world system.

Does 'Come out of her' mean physically leaving? Jer 51:45MSG | NIV | NRSVue commanded exiles to flee the literal city.
