Session 3: Revelation 6:1-8:5

For a participant: offer these when there is an opening, not as a lecture. Full session study

Parallel snippet, Rev 7:9

The Message 7:9
I looked again. I saw a huge crowd, too huge to count. Everyone was ...
NIV 7:9
The Great Multitude in White RobesAfter this I looked, and there before me was ...
NRSVue 7:9
The Multitude from Every Nation9 After this I looked, and there was a great ...

Evans: Dispensationalists distinguish the 144,000 (Jewish) from the multitude (tribulation converts); Evans likely follows this (inferred from his dispensational, kingdom-agenda framework).

Agree or differ: Agree on the global scope of salvation; differ on whether 144,000 and multitude are one group.

Red marks the words of Jesus.

Insights to offer

1. Greek, 'How long?' The martyrs cry heōs pote (6:10MSG | NIV | NRSVue), the lament of Pss 13MSG | NIV | NRSVue and 79. They are not rebuked; they are given white robes and told to rest a little longer.

2. Structure, the question and answer. The sixth seal ends with 'who is able to stand?' (6:17MSG | NIV | NRSVue). Chapter 7 answers before the seventh seal: the sealed people of God, a multitude from every nation, standing before the throne.

3. OT echo, sealing. The seal on the foreheads (7:3MSG | NIV | NRSVue) comes from Ezek 9:4MSG | NIV | NRSVue, where those who grieve over idolatry are marked for protection.

4. Roman context, prices. 6:6MSG | NIV | NRSVue puts a day's wheat at a day's wage while oil and wine are spared: luxuries flow while bread is scarce, possibly echoing Domitian's vine edict of AD 92.

Steering questions

  • The martyrs ask 'How long?' (6:10MSG | NIV | NRSVue) and are told to rest a little longer. Where are you waiting on God for justice?
  • In 6:15-17MSG | NIV | NRSVue kings, generals and the rich hide from the Lamb. Why does power make facing God harder?
  • The multitude of 7:9MSG | NIV | NRSVue is from every nation. What does that ask of our group's friendships and witness?
  • What do the martyrs' 'How long?' and the sealed multitude say to people who are suffering now?

Bridging a tangent

  • On dates and headlines: 'Christians have read this differently for 2,000 years. What do all the views agree it asks of us?'
  • On a long debate: 'Whatever we decide about that, where does the text land for us this week?'
  • On a personal story: 'That connects to 6:10MSG | NIV | NRSVue. Can we look at it together?'
  • On fear or anxiety: 'Notice who is on the throne in this passage. It is written to steady us.'
  • Handing back: 'I'll stop there. [Leader], where did you want to go next?'

If someone asks

Who is the rider on the white horse (6:2)? Some identify him with Christ (19:11MSG | NIV | NRSVue) or the gospel's advance; most recent commentators see a false or military conqueror, since he is one of four riders bringing judgment and his bow suggests the Parthians. Evans (inferred from his dispensational stance): often identified as the coming Antichrist, a counterfeit Christ who conquers by false peace.

Who are the 144,000? Views: (1) a literal Jewish remnant in the end times; (2) Jewish Christians; (3) the whole people of God described as the true Israel, the same group as the countless multitude of 7:9MSG | NIV | NRSVue (Bauckham, Beale), John 'hearing' 144,000 and 'seeing' a multitude as he heard 'Lion' and saw a Lamb. Evans (inferred from his dispensational stance): literal Jewish evangelists sealed during the tribulation.

What is 'the great tribulation' (7:14)? Futurists read a specific final period of seven years (often from Dan 9:27MSG | NIV | NRSVue). Evans (inferred from his dispensational stance): the second half of a future seven-year tribulation.
