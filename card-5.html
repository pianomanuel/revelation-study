Session 5: Revelation 11-12

For a participant: offer these when there is an opening, not as a lecture. Full session study

Parallel snippet, Rev 12:11

The Message 12:11
War broke out in Heaven. Michael and his Angels fought the Dragon. The Dragon ...
NIV 12:11
They triumphed over him by the blood of the Lamb and by the word ...
NRSVue 12:11
But they have conquered him by the blood of the Lamb and by the ...

Evans: Evans would apply 12:11MSG | NIV | NRSVue directly to spiritual warfare today (inferred from his dispensational, kingdom-agenda framework).

Agree or differ: Agree on the present application, whatever the timeline.

Red marks the words of Jesus.

Insights to offer

1. OT echo, lampstands. The two witnesses are 'the two lampstands' (11:4MSG | NIV | NRSVue; Zech 4MSG | NIV | NRSVue), and 1:20MSG | NIV | NRSVue says lampstands are churches. Their powers echo Moses and Elijah.

2. Structure, the center. 11:15MSG | NIV | NRSVue is the book's theological center: 'The kingdom of the world has become the kingdom of our Lord and of his Messiah.'

3. Greek, 'accuser'. Katēgōr (12:10MSG | NIV | NRSVue) is a legal term, the role of Satan in Job 1-2MSG | NIV | NRSVue and Zech 3MSG | NIV | NRSVue. He is thrown down, and the believers conquer him by the Lamb's blood and their testimony (12:11MSG | NIV | NRSVue).

4. Roman context, the dragon story. Chapter 12 retells the myth of Leto, Apollo and the dragon Python, which Nero used to cast himself as Apollo. John's version puts the emperor on the dragon's side.

Steering questions

  • The two witnesses are killed and the world celebrates (11:10MSG | NIV | NRSVue). How do you handle being mocked for faithfulness?
  • 12:11MSG | NIV | NRSVue: the blood of the Lamb, the word of testimony, and not clinging to life. Which of the three is weakest in you?
  • The dragon is furious 'because he knows his time is short' (12:12MSG | NIV | NRSVue). How does that change how you read spiritual attack?
  • How do the witnesses conquer (11:7-12MSG | NIV | NRSVue; 12:11MSG | NIV | NRSVue)? Where does that pattern show up in our lives?

Bridging a tangent

  • On dates and headlines: 'Christians have read this differently for 2,000 years. What do all the views agree it asks of us?'
  • On a long debate: 'Whatever we decide about that, where does the text land for us this week?'
  • On a personal story: 'That connects to 12:11MSG | NIV | NRSVue. Can we look at it together?'
  • On fear or anxiety: 'Notice who is on the throne in this passage. It is written to steady us.'
  • Handing back: 'I'll stop there. [Leader], where did you want to go next?'

If someone asks

Who are the two witnesses? Views: two literal end-time prophets (often Moses and Elijah, or Enoch and Elijah); Peter and Paul (some preterists); or the whole witnessing church, since they are called 'the two lampstands' and lampstands are churches (1:20MSG | NIV | NRSVue). Evans (inferred from his dispensational stance): two literal prophets in Jerusalem during the tribulation.

Who is the woman of chapter 12? Main readings: Israel (Gen 37:9MSG | NIV | NRSVue, sun, moon and stars; the twelve tribes); the people of God as one community, Israel bearing the Messiah and then the church; Mary, in later Catholic devotion. Evans (inferred from his dispensational stance): national Israel, protected during the tribulation.

Is the 1,260 days literal? The same period appears as 42 months and 'time, times and half a time' (Dan 7:25MSG | NIV | NRSVue), all three and a half years: a limited time of persecution. Evans (inferred from his dispensational stance): literal days in the tribulation.
