Revelation 22:6-21

Watch on RightNow Media · Read the chapter in the Bible App: MSG NIV NRSVue

The Message MSG

(22:20) He who testifies to all these things says it again: “I’m on my way! I’ll be there soon!” Yes! Come, Master Jesus!

New International Version NIV

(22:20) He who testifies to these things says, “Yes, I am coming soon.” Amen. Come, Lord Jesus.

NRSV Updated Edition NRSVue

(22:20) The one who testifies to these things says, “Surely I am coming soon.” Amen. Come, Lord Jesus!

Greek: tachy ('soon', 22:7, 12, 20); makarios ('blessed', 22:7 and 22:14, the 6th and 7th of the book's seven beatitudes); Marana tha equivalent ('Come, Lord Jesus').

OT allusions: Isa 40:10 and 62:11 ('his reward with him'); Isa 55:1 (the water of life without price); Deut 4:2 and 12:32 (do not add or take away); Num 24:17 and Isa 11:1 (root and star).

Roman context: The warning in 22:18-19 echoes covenant curse formulas and ancient textual-integrity clauses.

Interpretive schools: 22:14 textual note: 'wash their robes' (plynontes tas stolas autōn: Aleph, A) versus the TR's 'do his commandments', two similar-looking Greek phrases. 22:19 'book of life' (TR) is Erasmus's back-translation, as noted. Futurists and preterists both have to explain 'soon': imminence (could happen at any time), prophetic perspective, or first-century fulfilment.

Conclusions: The book ends the way it began, as a liturgical letter: beatitude, warning, invitation and grace. The church's response to the whole vision is prayer ('Come') and invitation ('Let everyone who is thirsty come').

Discussion questions

Men's group (Monday)

1. Jesus says 'I am coming soon' three times. How would you live this week if you took that seriously?

2. 'Let everyone who is thirsty come' (22:17). Which man in your life needs that invitation?

3. 22:18-19 warns against adding to or taking away from the prophecy. How do we handle speculation about the end responsibly?

Co-ed group (Tuesday)

1. 'Come, Lord Jesus' (22:20): is that prayer easy or hard for you right now?

2. Who would you most like to invite with 22:17, 'Let everyone who is thirsty come'?

3. What is one way this study of Revelation has changed how you see the future?
