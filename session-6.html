Revelation 13-14

Watch on RightNow Media · Read the chapter in the Bible App: MSG NIV NRSVue

The Message MSG

(14:12) Meanwhile, the saints stand passionately patient, keeping God’s commands, staying faithful to Jesus.

New International Version NIV

(14:12) This calls for patient endurance on the part of the people of God who keep his commands and remain faithful to Jesus.

NRSV Updated Edition NRSVue

(14:12) Here is a call for the endurance of the saints, those who keep the commandments of God and hold fast to the faith of Jesus.

Greek: thērion ('beast'); charagma ('mark'; also used of the imperial stamp on documents and coins); psēphisatō ('let him calculate', 13:18, gematria).

OT allusions: Dan 7:3-8 (the four beasts combined into one); Dan 3 (worship the image or die); Isa 21:9 ('Fallen is Babylon', 14:8); Joel 3:13 (harvest and winepress).

Roman context: The sea beast represents imperial power arriving from overseas. The land beast is the provincial priesthood of the imperial cult (koinon of Asia). 666 is gematria for 'Nero Caesar' in Hebrew letters (nrwn qsr). A variant reading 616 (P115, C; known to Irenaeus) matches the Latin spelling 'Nero'. That explanation is the scholarly consensus (Bauckham, Aune, Koester).

Interpretive schools: Futurists expect a personal Antichrist and a literal mark. Idealists and preterists read imperial and economic coercion recurring in every age. Note the contrast: the mark of the beast is a parody of God's seal on the foreheads (7:3; 14:1).

Conclusions: The section ends with a call to 'endurance' (14:12) and a beatitude (14:13). The question for the reader is allegiance, not decoding.

Discussion questions

Men's group (Monday)

1. The beast controls buying and selling (13:16-17). Where does economic pressure test your allegiance at work?

2. 'Who is like the beast?' (13:4) parodies 'Who is like God?' What do men today admire with near-worship?

3. 14:12 calls for endurance from the saints. What concrete habit keeps you faithful over the long haul?

Co-ed group (Tuesday)

1. 13:16-17 pictures pressure to conform in order to buy, sell and belong. Where do women and men in our group feel that pressure differently?

2. 14:13 blesses those who die in the Lord. How has the faith of someone you lost shaped you?

3. What helps our group practice the 'endurance' of 14:12 together?
