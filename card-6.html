Session 6: Revelation 13-14

For a participant: offer these when there is an opening, not as a lecture. Full session study

Parallel snippet, Rev 14:12

The Message 14:12
Meanwhile, the saints stand passionately patient, keeping God’s commands, staying faithful to Jesus.
NIV 14:12
This calls for patient endurance on the part of the people of God who ...
NRSVue 14:12
Here is a call for the endurance of the saints, those who keep the ...

Evans: Evans stresses faithfulness: 'Faithfulness means consistency over time' (Evans, sermon 'God's Prophetic Calendar' (transcript, sermons.love)).

Agree or differ: Strong agreement.

Red marks the words of Jesus.

Insights to offer

1. Greek, 'mark'. Charagma (13:16MSG | NIV | NRSVue) was the word for the imperial stamp on contracts and coins. It parodies God's seal on the foreheads (7:3MSG | NIV | NRSVue): the question is allegiance.

2. OT echo, Daniel. The sea beast combines all four of Daniel's beasts (Dan 7:3-7MSG | NIV | NRSVue) into one, and the command to worship its image echoes Dan 3MSG | NIV | NRSVue.

3. Roman context, 666. 'Nero Caesar' in Hebrew letters totals 666; the Latin spelling totals 616, a variant known to Irenaeus. Most scholars accept this as the first-century referent.

4. Structure, parody. The beast's healed death wound (13:3MSG | NIV | NRSVue) mimics the Lamb 'as though slaughtered' (5:6MSG | NIV | NRSVue), and 'Who is like the beast?' (13:4MSG | NIV | NRSVue) mimics 'Who is like you, O LORD?' (Exod 15:11MSG | NIV | NRSVue).

Steering questions

  • The beast controls buying and selling (13:16-17MSG | NIV | NRSVue). Where does economic pressure test your allegiance at work?
  • 'Who is like the beast?' (13:4MSG | NIV | NRSVue) parodies 'Who is like God?' What do men today admire with near-worship?
  • 14:12MSG | NIV | NRSVue calls for endurance from the saints. What concrete habit keeps you faithful over the long haul?
  • Where are we asked for allegiance that belongs to God? What does endurance (14:12MSG | NIV | NRSVue) look like this week?

Bridging a tangent

  • On dates and headlines: 'Christians have read this differently for 2,000 years. What do all the views agree it asks of us?'
  • On a long debate: 'Whatever we decide about that, where does the text land for us this week?'
  • On a personal story: 'That connects to 13:16-17MSG | NIV | NRSVue. Can we look at it together?'
  • On fear or anxiety: 'Notice who is on the throne in this passage. It is written to steady us.'
  • Handing back: 'I'll stop there. [Leader], where did you want to go next?'

If someone asks

What is the mark of the beast? The Greek charagma was used for the imperial stamp on contracts and the image on coins; in Revelation it parodies God's seal on the foreheads (7:3MSG | NIV | NRSVue). Evans (inferred from his dispensational stance): a literal future mark required for buying and selling.

What does 666 mean? 'Nero Caesar' spelled in Hebrew letters totals 666; spelled from the Latin form it totals 616, a variant known to Irenaeus and found in P115. Evans (inferred from his dispensational stance): the number of a future Antichrist, to be identified only when he appears.

Who is the Antichrist? The word 'antichrist' appears only in 1-2 John, not in Revelation; the beast, the man of lawlessness (2 Thess 2MSG | NIV | NRSVue) and Daniel's little horn are often combined into one figure. Evans (inferred from his dispensational stance): a future individual world ruler.
